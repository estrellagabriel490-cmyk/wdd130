History

Founded with a passion for adrenaline and nature, our company has been guiding thrilling whitewater adventures for over two decades. We pride ourselves on safety, unforgettable experiences, and protecting our natural waterways.

From humble beginnings with a single raft, we have grown into a premier adventure company serving thousands of thrill-seekers each year. Join us to create your own story!
